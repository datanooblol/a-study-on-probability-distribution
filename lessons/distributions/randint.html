Discrete

Discrete Uniform

scipy.stats.randint

Intuition

Every whole number in a range is exactly equally likely -- no favorites. It's the discrete cousin of the continuous Uniform distribution, and the simplest possible 'fair' randomness.

Formula

\[ P(X=k) = \frac{1}{\text{high} - \text{low}}, \quad k = \text{low}, \ldots, \text{high}-1 \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning
low1smallest possible value (inclusive)
high7one past the largest possible value

Use cases

  • Simulating fair dice or lottery draws
  • Randomly sampling an index/ID from a fixed range
  • Shuffling and permutation tests in statistics

A funny example

Rolling one ordinary, perfectly fair six-sided die.

What's the chance of rolling a 4, and what's the chance of rolling a 4 or higher?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.randint(low=1, high=7)
print('P(roll a 4):', round(dist.pmf(4), 4))
print('P(roll 4 or higher):', round(dist.sf(3), 4))
P(roll a 4): 0.1667 P(roll 4 or higher): 0.5
