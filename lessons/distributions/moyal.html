Moyal

scipy.stats.moyal

Intuition

Moyal's distribution is a close, easier-to-compute approximation to the Landau distribution, describing energy loss of a fast charged particle through matter -- it has a sharp rise, a peak, and an exponentially-decaying tail, shaped like a smoothed-out asymmetric bump.

Formula

\[ f(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\exp\!\left(-\frac{1}{2}\left(x+e^{-x}\right)\right) \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning

Use cases

  • Approximating energy loss (ionization) distributions in particle detectors
  • Radiation and high-energy physics fluctuation modeling

A funny example

A marble rolls through a row of soft pillows, losing a bit of speed at each one. Most of the time it loses a similar typical amount, and this shape captures the slight lean toward occasionally losing a bit more.

What's the probability the energy loss falls between -1 and 1?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.moyal()
print('P(-1 < X < 1):', round(dist.cdf(1) - dist.cdf(-1), 4))
print('mean:', round(dist.mean(), 4))
P(-1 < X < 1): 0.445 mean: 1.2704
