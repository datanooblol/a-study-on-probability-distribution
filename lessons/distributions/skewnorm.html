Skew-Normal

scipy.stats.skewnorm

Intuition

Skew-normal takes the familiar bell curve and tilts it, letting one tail stretch out further than the other while keeping most of the normal distribution's convenient mathematics -- exactly what you want when data is roughly bell-shaped but has a noticeable lean.

Formula

\[ f(x;a) = 2\,\phi(x)\,\Phi(ax) \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning
a4skewness parameter (0 recovers the ordinary normal distribution)

Use cases

  • Modeling skewed near-normal biological and physical measurements
  • Selection-effect modeling in econometrics and biostatistics

A funny example

A kid measures how many minutes late the school bus is each morning. It's almost never early, occasionally exactly on time, and most often just a couple minutes late -- but some mornings it's dramatically late, giving the timing a distinct rightward lean.

What's the probability the bus is more than 1 unit late (in standardized minutes)?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.skewnorm(a=4)
print('P(X > 1):', round(dist.sf(1), 4))
print('mean:', round(dist.mean(), 4))
P(X > 1): 0.3173 mean: 0.7741
