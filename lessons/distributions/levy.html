Levy

scipy.stats.levy

Intuition

One of only a few stable distributions with a known closed-form pdf, Levy is extremely heavy-tailed -- it has infinite mean and variance -- and describes the first time a simple random walk returns to its starting point, which is why it also appears at the heart of Levy flight foraging models in biology.

Formula

\[ f(x) = \sqrt{\frac{1}{2\pi x^3}}\exp\!\left(-\frac{1}{2x}\right), \quad x > 0 \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning

Use cases

  • Levy flight foraging models in animal movement ecology
  • First-return-time modeling for one-dimensional random walks

A funny example

A hungry seagull hops around a beach looking for food, mostly taking short hops but every so often taking one wildly long hop far across the sand -- this unpredictable mix of mostly-short-but-occasionally-huge hops is a Levy flight, powered by the Levy distribution.

What's the probability the seagull's next hop distance exceeds 3 units?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.levy()
print('P(hop distance > 3):', round(dist.sf(3), 4))
print('median hop distance:', round(dist.median(), 4))
P(hop distance > 3): 0.4363 median hop distance: 2.1981
