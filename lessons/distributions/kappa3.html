Kappa 3

scipy.stats.kappa3

Intuition

A flexible three-parameter-family member (here with location/scale fixed) developed by Mielke and Johnson for meteorological data, particularly precipitation amounts, since it can capture a wide range of skewness with just one free shape parameter.

Formula

\[ f(x;a) = a\left(a+x^a\right)^{-(a+1)/a}, \quad x > 0 \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning
a2shape parameter

Use cases

  • Precipitation and meteorological extreme data modeling
  • Flexible skewed-data fitting with a single shape parameter

A funny example

A weather station records how much rain falls during each storm. Most storms drop a modest, similar amount, but the kappa-3 shape's single knob lets meteorologists tune exactly how often a storm dumps way more rain than usual.

What's the probability a storm's rainfall exceeds 2 units?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.kappa3(a=2)
print('P(rainfall > 2):', round(dist.sf(2), 4))
print('mean rainfall:', round(dist.mean(), 4))
P(rainfall > 2): 0.1835 mean rainfall: 1.4142
