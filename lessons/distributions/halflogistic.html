Half-Logistic

scipy.stats.halflogistic

Intuition

The positive half of the logistic distribution, folded at zero -- it behaves like a slightly heavier-tailed cousin of the half-normal, popular in reliability engineering because its hazard rate increases in a simple, well-understood way.

Formula

\[ f(x) = \frac{2e^{-x}}{(1+e^{-x})^2}, \quad x \ge 0 \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning

Use cases

  • Reliability and survival analysis with increasing hazard rate
  • Modeling positive-only residuals in regression diagnostics

A funny example

A kid's toy drone can only fly a positive distance from the launch pad before it needs to land, and how far it typically gets follows this shape -- shorter flights are common, longer ones taper off but not too abruptly.

What's the probability the drone flies farther than 2 units?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.halflogistic()
print('P(X > 2):', round(dist.sf(2), 4))
print('median:', round(dist.median(), 4))
P(X > 2): 0.2384 median: 1.0986
