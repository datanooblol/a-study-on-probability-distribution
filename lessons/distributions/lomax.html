Lomax (Pareto Type II)

scipy.stats.lomax

Intuition

Lomax is just a Pareto distribution shifted so it starts at zero instead of one -- it's the classic model for 'excess over a threshold' waiting times and losses, and it has the memoryless-ish property that its hazard rate decreases over time (unlike the constant-hazard exponential).

Formula

\[ f(x;c) = \frac{c}{(1+x)^{c+1}}, \quad x \ge 0 \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning
c2shape parameter (tail index)

Use cases

  • Insurance claim size and reinsurance excess-of-loss modeling
  • Internet traffic file-size and web-request duration modeling

A funny example

A kid times how long it takes for their pet hamster to come out of its hiding tube once called. Most times are short, but the longer the hamster has already been hiding, the more likely it is to keep hiding even longer -- the wait times pile up in this Lomax shape.

What's the probability the hamster takes longer than 3 (scaled) time units to emerge?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.lomax(c=2)
print('P(wait > 3):', round(dist.sf(3), 4))
print('mean wait:', round(dist.mean(), 4))
P(wait > 3): 0.0625 mean wait: 1.0
