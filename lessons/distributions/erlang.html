Erlang

scipy.stats.erlang

Intuition

Erlang is the waiting time until the a-th event of a Poisson process happens -- it's the sum of a independent exponential wait times, invented by Agner Erlang to model queueing at telephone exchanges.

Formula

\[ f(x;a) = \frac{x^{a-1}e^{-x}}{\Gamma(a)}, \quad x \ge 0 \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning
a3number of independent exponential stages, k (an integer count)

Use cases

  • Queueing theory: waiting time for the k-th arrival/service
  • Telecom call-center and network traffic modeling

A funny example

A kid is waiting in line for the ice cream truck, and it takes a random exponential amount of time between each of the 3 customers ahead of them being served. The Erlang distribution tells you how long the kid waits until it's finally their turn.

What's the probability the kid waits more than 5 minutes, and what's the expected wait?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.erlang(a=3)
print('P(wait > 5 min):', round(dist.sf(5), 4))
print('expected wait:', round(dist.mean(), 4))
P(wait > 5 min): 0.1247 expected wait: 3.0
