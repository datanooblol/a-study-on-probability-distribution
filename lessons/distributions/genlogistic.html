Generalized Logistic

scipy.stats.genlogistic

Intuition

Generalized logistic stretches one tail of the ordinary (symmetric) logistic distribution, letting it model skewed data while keeping the logistic's convenient, simple algebraic form -- often used in hydrology for modeling skewed rainfall extremes.

Formula

\[ f(x;c) = \frac{c\,e^{-x}}{(1+e^{-x})^{c+1}}, \quad x \in \mathbb{R} \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning
c2shape parameter controlling skewness

Use cases

  • Hydrology: modeling skewed rainfall and flood extremes
  • Skewed alternative to the logistic distribution in growth modeling

A funny example

A kid measures how much rain falls each week. Most weeks are near-average, but the amount can spike up much more easily than it can spike down, giving the distribution a lopsided long tail on the wet side.

What's the probability rainfall exceeds 2 units in a given week, and what's the expected amount?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.genlogistic(c=2)
print('P(rainfall > 2):', round(dist.sf(2), 4))
print('expected rainfall:', round(dist.mean(), 4))
P(rainfall > 2): 0.2242 expected rainfall: 1.0
