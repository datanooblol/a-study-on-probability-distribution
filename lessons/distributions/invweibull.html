Inverse Weibull (Frechet)

scipy.stats.invweibull

Intuition

Also known as the Frechet distribution, this is one of the three classic extreme-value families and describes the distribution of the maximum of samples drawn from a distribution with a heavy power-law tail -- think record-breaking rainfall or record stock gains.

Formula

\[ f(x;c) = c\,x^{-c-1}e^{-x^{-c}}, \quad x > 0 \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning
c3shape parameter controlling tail heaviness

Use cases

  • Extreme value theory for maxima of heavy-tailed data (Frechet family)
  • Modeling maximum wind speeds and flood peaks in climate risk

A funny example

A kid tracks the single biggest wave that rolls in each day at the beach. Most days the biggest wave is a fairly typical size, but the Frechet shape leaves real room for an occasional monster wave far bigger than the rest.

What's the probability the day's biggest wave exceeds 2 units?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.invweibull(c=3)
print('P(wave > 2):', round(dist.sf(2), 4))
print('median wave size:', round(dist.median(), 4))
P(wave > 2): 0.1175 median wave size: 1.1299
