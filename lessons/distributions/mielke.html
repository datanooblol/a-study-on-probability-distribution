Mielke (Dagum)

scipy.stats.mielke

Intuition

Also known as the Dagum distribution in economics, this is a close cousin of the Burr distribution used specifically to model personal income -- it can capture the realistic shape where most people earn a moderate amount but income spreads into a long, heavy right tail.

Formula

\[ f(x;k,s) = \frac{k\,x^{k-1}}{(1+x^s)^{1+k/s}}, \quad x > 0 \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning
k4shape parameter controlling the rise near zero
s3shape parameter controlling the tail decay

Use cases

  • Personal income distribution modeling in economics (Dagum distribution)
  • Precipitation and rainfall amount modeling

A funny example

A kid surveys allowance amounts across their whole school. Most kids get a similar, modest allowance, but a few get quite a bit more -- this distribution is the economist's favorite tool for describing exactly that kind of income spread.

What's the probability an allowance exceeds 1.5 (scaled) units?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.mielke(k=4, s=3)
print('P(allowance > 1.5):', round(dist.sf(1.5), 4))
print('median allowance:', round(dist.median(), 4))
P(allowance > 1.5): 0.2925 median allowance: 1.1362
