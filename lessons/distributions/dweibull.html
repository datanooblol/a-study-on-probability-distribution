Double Weibull

scipy.stats.dweibull

Intuition

Double Weibull mirrors a Weibull distribution around zero, producing a symmetric distribution whose peakedness (pointy vs. flat-topped) is tunable through the shape parameter c, unlike the fixed shape of the normal curve.

Formula

\[ f(x;c) = \frac{c}{2}|x|^{c-1}e^{-|x|^c}, \quad x \in \mathbb{R} \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning
c1.5shape parameter of each one-sided Weibull half

Use cases

  • Symmetric residual/error modeling with tunable peakedness
  • Signal and vibration data with non-Gaussian symmetric noise

A funny example

A dart lands left or right of the bullseye's vertical line with equal chance, and how far off it lands follows a Weibull-shaped spread on each side -- sometimes clustered tight near the line, sometimes with a pointier peak than a normal dartboard error would give.

What's the probability the dart lands more than 1.5 units from the line, on either side?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.dweibull(c=1.5)
print('P(|offset| > 1.5):', round(2 * dist.sf(1.5), 4))
print('variance:', round(dist.var(), 4))
P(|offset| > 1.5): 0.1593 variance: 1.1906
