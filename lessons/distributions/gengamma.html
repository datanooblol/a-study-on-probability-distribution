Generalized Gamma

scipy.stats.gengamma

Intuition

Generalized gamma adds a power-transform knob c on top of the ordinary gamma distribution, letting it mimic gamma, Weibull, exponential, and half-normal distributions all as special cases just by adjusting a and c.

Formula

\[ f(x;a,c) = \frac{|c|\,x^{ca-1}e^{-x^c}}{\Gamma(a)}, \quad x > 0 \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning
a5shape parameter (like gamma's shape)
c2power-transform shape parameter

Use cases

  • Flexible lifetime/survival modeling that nests Weibull and gamma as special cases
  • Hydrology: modeling rainfall and streamflow amounts

A funny example

A vending machine dispenses candy after a random wait, and by tuning two knobs -- how many 'stages' of waiting (a) and how the wait stretches or compresses (c) -- you can make the machine behave like several different familiar waiting-time toys, all with one master dial.

What's the probability the wait exceeds 2 units, and what's the expected wait?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.gengamma(a=5, c=2)
print('P(wait > 2):', round(dist.sf(2), 4))
print('expected wait:', round(dist.mean(), 4))
P(wait > 2): 0.6288 expected wait: 2.1809
