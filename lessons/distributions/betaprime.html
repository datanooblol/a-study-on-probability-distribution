Beta Prime

scipy.stats.betaprime

Intuition

Beta prime is what you get when you take the odds ratio p/(1-p) of a Beta-distributed probability p -- it lives on all positive numbers instead of just [0,1], making it useful whenever you care about a ratio of two positive random quantities.

Formula

\[ f(x;a,b) = \frac{x^{a-1}(1+x)^{-a-b}}{B(a,b)}, \quad x > 0 \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning
a5first shape parameter, alpha
b6second shape parameter, beta

Use cases

  • Modeling ratios of independent gamma-distributed quantities
  • Bayesian priors for variance ratios and odds ratios

A funny example

Two friends split a bag of marbles randomly between them using a Beta-distributed split. If you instead ask 'how many times more marbles does friend A have than friend B,' that ratio follows a Beta Prime distribution.

What's the probability friend A has less than twice as many marbles as friend B, and what's the expected ratio?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.betaprime(a=5, b=6)
print('P(ratio < 2):', round(dist.cdf(2), 4))
print('expected ratio:', round(dist.mean(), 4))
P(ratio < 2): 0.9234 expected ratio: 1.0
