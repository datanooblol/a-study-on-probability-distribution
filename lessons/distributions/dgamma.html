Double Gamma

scipy.stats.dgamma

Intuition

Double gamma glues two mirror-image gamma distributions together at zero, giving a symmetric distribution with a sharper peak (or flatter middle) and heavier tails than the normal, depending on shape a.

Formula

\[ f(x;a) = \frac{1}{2\Gamma(a)}|x|^{a-1}e^{-|x|}, \quad x \in \mathbb{R} \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning
a2shape parameter of each one-sided gamma half

Use cases

  • Symmetric heavy-tailed error modeling as an alternative to the normal
  • Signal processing residuals with peaked, fat-tailed noise

A funny example

A see-saw is perfectly balanced, and how far it tips left or right each time follows the same 'gamma' pattern on both sides -- equally likely to tip left as right, but with a distinctive peaked-and-tailed shape rather than a plain bell curve.

What's the probability the see-saw tips more than 2 units to either side?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.dgamma(a=2)
print('P(|tip| > 2):', round(2 * dist.sf(2), 4))
print('variance:', round(dist.var(), 4))
P(|tip| > 2): 0.406 variance: 6.0
