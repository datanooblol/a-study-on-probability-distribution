Studentized Range

scipy.stats.studentized_range

Intuition

This is the distribution of the range (max minus min) of k sample means divided by their estimated standard error -- the exact tool behind Tukey's HSD post-hoc test, which compares every pair of group means after an ANOVA without inflating the false-positive rate.

Formula

\[ f(x;k,\nu) = \frac{k(k-1)\nu^{\nu/2}}{\Gamma(\nu/2)2^{\nu/2-1}}\int_0^\infty\!\int_{-\infty}^\infty s^\nu e^{-\nu s^2/2}\phi(z)\phi(sx+z)\left[\Phi(sx+z)-\Phi(z)\right]^{k-2}dz\,ds \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning
k3number of groups/samples being compared
df10degrees of freedom for the variance estimate

Use cases

  • Tukey's HSD post-hoc multiple comparison test after ANOVA
  • Quality control range-chart statistical process control

A funny example

A science class splits into 3 teams to grow plants under different lights, then compares the tallest team's average against the shortest team's average. This distribution tells you how big that gap needs to be before it's more than what random luck alone would explain.

What's the probability the range between the tallest and shortest team averages exceeds 4 (in standardized units)?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.studentized_range(k=3, df=10)
print('P(range > 4):', round(dist.sf(4), 4))
print('mean range:', round(dist.mean(), 4))
P(range > 4): 0.0433 mean range: 1.8343
