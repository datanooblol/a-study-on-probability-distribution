Generalized Inverse Gaussian

scipy.stats.geninvgauss

Intuition

This family generalizes the inverse Gaussian distribution with an extra power-law knob p, and shows up naturally as the distribution of first-passage-type quantities in physics and as a mixing distribution in Bayesian hierarchical models.

Formula

\[ f(x;p,b) = \frac{x^{p-1}}{2K_p(b)}\exp\!\left(-\frac{b}{2}\left(x+\frac1x\right)\right), \quad x > 0 \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning
p1shape parameter (can be any real number)
b2shape parameter, b > 0

Use cases

  • Bayesian hierarchical models as a mixing/prior distribution
  • First-passage-time modeling in physics and finance

A funny example

A hamster runs on a wheel and its exact 'burst of energy level' at any moment follows this two-knob distribution -- it's rarely near zero or extremely huge, clustering instead around a typical energy level shaped by both knobs.

What's the probability the hamster's energy level exceeds 3, and what's its expected level?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.geninvgauss(p=1, b=2)
print('P(X > 3):', round(dist.sf(3), 4))
print('mean:', round(dist.mean(), 4))
P(X > 3): 0.1371 mean: 1.8143
