Cosine

scipy.stats.cosine

Intuition

The cosine distribution is a smooth, bell-shaped alternative to the normal distribution on a bounded interval -- it's often used as a cheap, closed-form stand-in for the normal curve because it has similar shape but simple, exact formulas.

Formula

\[ f(x) = \frac{1}{2\pi}\left(1+\cos(x)\right), \quad -\pi \le x \le \pi \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning

Use cases

  • Closed-form approximation to the normal distribution
  • Directional/angular data smoothing

A funny example

A pendulum swings gently and you photograph its angle at a random moment. It's most often caught hovering near the middle of its swing and progressively rarer as you look toward the far edges.

What's the probability the pendulum's angle is caught within 1 radian of center?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.cosine()
print('P(-1 < X < 1):', round(dist.cdf(1) - dist.cdf(-1), 4))
print('variance:', round(dist.var(), 4))
P(-1 < X < 1): 0.5862 variance: 1.2899
