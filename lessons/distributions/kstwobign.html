Kolmogorov (asymptotic two-sided KS)

scipy.stats.kstwobign

Intuition

This is the limiting shape of the two-sided KS statistic as the sample size n grows to infinity (specifically, sqrt(n) times the KS statistic converges to this) -- the classical Kolmogorov distribution used for large-sample goodness-of-fit testing.

Formula

\[ F(x) = 1 - 2\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{k-1}e^{-2k^2x^2}, \quad x > 0 \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning

Use cases

  • Large-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov goodness-of-fit testing
  • Asymptotic theory for empirical process convergence

A funny example

Instead of just 20 die rolls, imagine the kid rolls the die a million times. The scaled biggest gap between their tally and the ideal fair-die chart settles down into this one universal shape, no matter how many rolls they eventually do (as long as it's a lot).

What's the probability the scaled statistic exceeds 1.2?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.kstwobign()
print('P(X > 1.2):', round(dist.sf(1.2), 4))
print('median:', round(dist.median(), 4))
P(X > 1.2): 0.1122 median: 0.8276
