Burr (Type XII, Burr-12 mirrored)

scipy.stats.burr

Intuition

The Burr distribution is a flexible two-shape-parameter family widely used to fit income, wealth, and insurance-loss data because it can produce a wide range of skewness and tail-heaviness just by tuning c and d.

Formula

\[ f(x;c,d) = c\,d\,x^{-c-1}(1+x^{-c})^{-d-1}, \quad x > 0 \]
parameterdefault used heremeaning
c10.5shape parameter controlling the tail's power-law decay rate
d4.3second shape parameter controlling density near the origin

Use cases

  • Income and wealth distribution modeling in economics
  • Insurance claim size and reliability/failure-time modeling

A funny example

A kid collects trading cards and tracks how many hours each card took to trade away. Most cards trade fast, but the c and d knobs on this distribution let statisticians dial in exactly how many stubborn, slow-to-trade cards show up in the long tail.

What's the probability a card takes more than 2 hours to trade, and what's the median trading time?

Solve it with scipy.stats

import scipy.stats as stats
import numpy as np

dist = stats.burr(c=10.5, d=4.3)
print('P(X > 2):', round(dist.sf(2), 4))
print('median:', round(dist.median(), 4))
P(X > 2): 0.003 median: 1.1806
